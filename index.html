About Us

Pippilo Ice Creams is a homegrown ice cream brand that began its journey in 2019 with a simple dream and a bicycle. What started as a small cycle-based distribution soon grew into a trusted local brand loved for its taste and quality. Our story began at home, where we carefully manufactured our products using passion, creativity, and a commitment to freshness. With limited resources but unlimited dedication, we started selling our ice creams locally, directly reaching customers and building strong relationships within the community. Pippilo Ice Creams officially took shape when we joined hands with SIP UP, marking an important milestone in our growth. This partnership helped us expand our reach while continuing to maintain the homemade quality and authentic flavors that define our brand. Today, Pippilo Ice Creams stands for happiness, freshness, and trust. Every scoop reflects our journey—from a humble beginning to a growing brand—while staying true to our roots and our promise to deliver delightful ice cream experiences.
